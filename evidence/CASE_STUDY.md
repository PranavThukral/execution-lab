## What to explain in an interview

Baseline: urgency reduces exposure to this rising buy path. Spread and impact remain modeled. Inspect attribution before choosing a schedule.

Liquidity squeeze: replay capacity bounds completion under this fill model. Residual shares matter even if filled-only slippage looks favorable.

Sell timing: reversing side changes signed timing/opportunity costs. A buy-path conclusion does not automatically transfer to selling.

The prior-session estimate cannot see evaluation volume; realized capacity can. A schedule can fail despite sufficient aggregate capacity because timing constrains access to liquidity.
